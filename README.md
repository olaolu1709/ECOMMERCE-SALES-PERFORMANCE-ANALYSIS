# E-Commerce Sales Performance Analysis 📊

## Project Overview

An e-commerce sales analysis project using **Microsoft Excel and Power BI** to identify key business trends, product performance, regional performance, and customer payment preferences.

The goal of this project was to transform raw sales data into actionable business insights that can support data-driven decision-making.

## Project Objective

The main objective of this project is to understand the factors driving e-commerce sales performance and provide insights that can support business decisions.

The analysis focuses on:

- Product category performance
- Regional revenue performance
- Customer payment preferences
- Monthly sales trends
- Order volume by product category
- Overall sales performance

## Dataset

**Dataset:** E-Commerce Sales Performance Analysis  
**Source:** Kaggle  
**Records:** 5,000 orders  
**Total Revenue:** Approximately 5.11M

## Business Questions

1. Which product categories generate the most revenue?
2. Which payment methods are most frequently used by customers?
3. Which regions contribute the most revenue?
4. What are the monthly sales trends?
5. Which product categories receive the most orders?

## ❓ Business Questions, Methods & Solutions

### 1. Which product categories generate the most revenue?

**Method:** PivotTable using Product Category and Sum of Revenue.

**Solution:** Electronics generated the highest revenue (1.83M), followed by Clothing (1.53M).

---

### 2. Which payment methods are most frequently used?

**Method:** PivotTable using Payment Method and Count of Orders.

**Solution:** Card was the most used payment method with 2,270 orders.

---

### 3. Which regions contribute the most revenue?

**Method:** PivotTable using Region and Sum of Revenue.

**Solution:** The West generated the highest revenue at approximately 1.35M.

---

### 4. What are the monthly sales trends?

**Method:** PivotTable with Order Date grouped by Month and Sum of Revenue, visualized with a Line Chart.

**Solution:** Revenue fluctuated across the months, with May recording the highest revenue and April the lowest.

---

### 5. Which product categories receive the most orders?

**Method:** PivotTable using Product Category and Count of Orders.

**Solution:** Electronics had the highest order volume with 1,777 orders, followed by Clothing with 1,531.

## Key Findings

- **Electronics** generated the highest revenue at approximately **1.83M**.
- **Clothing** was the second-highest revenue category at approximately **1.53M**.
- Electronics and Clothing together generated approximately **65.8% of total revenue**.
- **West** was the highest-revenue region at approximately **1.35M**.
- **Card** was the most frequently used payment method, accounting for **2,270 orders**.
- **Electronics** recorded the highest number of orders with **1,777 orders**.
- Monthly revenue fluctuated throughout the year, indicating opportunities to improve sales consistency.
- **Beauty** recorded the lowest order volume with **723 orders**.

## Business Recommendations

- Maintain strong inventory and marketing support for Electronics and Clothing.
- Investigate strategies to improve weaker-performing categories.
- Maintain strong performance in the West region while identifying opportunities in other regions.
- Ensure reliable Card and COD payment options.
- Investigate the causes of monthly sales fluctuations to improve revenue consistency.

## Business Conclusion

The analysis shows that the e-commerce business has a strong sales base, with Electronics and Clothing contributing the largest share of revenue and orders. The West region is the leading revenue contributor, while Card is the most preferred payment method. However, monthly revenue fluctuates and some categories perform below others. Management should maintain the strongest-performing areas while improving weaker categories and reducing sales fluctuations to achieve more consistent and sustainable growth.
